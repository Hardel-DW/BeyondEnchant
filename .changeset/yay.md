## Beyond Enchant - Minecraft 26.2
Beyond Enchant is now compatible with Minecraft 26.2.

### Update
- Pack format updated to 107 (26.2).
- All vanilla enchantment overrides migrated to the new namespaced data format introduced in 26.2 (`minecraft:entity_type`, `minecraft:type_specific`, `minecraft:location`, ...).
- Affected enchantments: Bane of Arthropods, Frost Walker, Impaling, Looting, Lunge, Power, Punch, Smite, Soul Speed and Wind Burst.

No gameplay change in this release, it is a compatibility update. Have fun, and as usual the mod version is available for those who prefer it.
